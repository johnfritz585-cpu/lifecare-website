Our Core Features

4 powerful modules designed for student success

💊

Health Management

Digital health profile with allergies, medications, and medical history. Track symptoms, habits, and get nutrition guidance.

🎯

Duty Preparation

Nursing students: Never be unprepared again. Duty checklists, area-based reviews, pre-duty reminders, and confidence tips.

📅

Task & Time Management

Organize your school and personal life. Daily planner, to-do lists, smart reminders, and priority tagging.

💳

Financial Tracker

Take control of your money. Log expenses, set budget limits, receive alerts, and get weekly/monthly summaries.
